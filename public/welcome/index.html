FROM “TO DO” TO “TA-DA!”

A little routine.
A happier rhythm.

  1. Do a chore. Show the effort.

    Kids complete a chore and submit details and a photo.

  2. Greenie helps. Parents have the final say.

    Greenie can provisionally approve a clear photo. Parents review everything else and can revoke any earned credit.

  3. Earn time. Celebrate the win.

    Approved chores earn credit. Parents approve screen time use. Milestones unlock virtual rewards.
